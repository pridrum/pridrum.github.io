Honored to receive the **2026 College of Engineering Outstanding Graduate Teaching Award (PhD)**
from Northeastern University — one of eight PhD recipients across the college, representing
Mechanical & Industrial Engineering.

The award was announced by Dean Sagar Kamarthi and celebrated at the COE Outstanding Graduate
Student Awards Ceremony on April 28, 2026 in the Raytheon Amphitheater, Egan Research Center.

Thank you to my advisor [Prof. Kayse Lee Maass](https://mie.northeastern.edu/people/maass-kayse-lee/),
[Prof. Hugh McManus](https://mie.northeastern.edu/people/mcmanus-hugh/), and every student who
trusted me with their learning.
