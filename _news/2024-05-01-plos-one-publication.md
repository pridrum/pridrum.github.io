Our paper **"Enhancing Detection of Labor Violations in the Agricultural Sector:
A Multilevel Generalized Linear Regression Model of H-2A Violation Counts"**
is now published in *PLOS ONE* (Vol. 19, No. 5, e0302960).

With co-authors Jafari, A., Bhimani, S., Nishigaya, D., Ding, A.A., Farrell, A., and Maass, K.L.,
this work develops a statistical model leveraging public data to improve inspection strategies
for detecting labor violations in U.S. agricultural supply chains — directly supporting
anti-labor trafficking efforts.

**DOI:** [10.1371/journal.pone.0302960](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0302960)
