I built these for an operations research course at UTRGV during the remote teaching transition,
when students had lost the part of the class where they could watch someone work a problem. Each
one solves a problem end to end rather than explaining a method in the abstract.

- [Tutorial 1](https://youtu.be/GOG1Yf72Pow)
- [Tutorial 2](https://youtu.be/gbPlL9ZMawA)
- [Tutorial 3](https://youtu.be/lSACgCrVY8w)
- [Tutorial 4](https://youtu.be/rQ6V6cQWGy8)

A student in OR 6205 asked for exactly this again in Spring 2026, in place of the textbook reading
that was not working. Replacing that reading with a short video series is one of the four changes I
am carrying into the next course I teach, on the [evaluations](/teaching/evaluations/) page.

A recorded teaching demonstration is available on request.
