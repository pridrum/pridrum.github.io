Research Interests

Publications

  • 2024
    Enhancing detection of labor violations in the agricultural sector: A multilevel generalized linear regression model of H-2A violation counts
    PLOS ONE, 19(5), e0302960 — refereed journal article

    A multilevel regression model of H-2A visa and labor violations, built to help the U.S. Department of Labor target limited investigative capacity toward the producers most associated with violations, including those that can indicate labor trafficking.

  • 2026
    Collaboration in anti-trafficking efforts: A network interdiction problem with multiple dependent attacks
    Job market paper — working paper, submission September 2026

    What happens to a coordinated enforcement decision when several agencies act on the same part of a network and their actions do not combine independently. Defines the class of functions satisfying four axioms rather than assuming one algebraic form, and gives an exact linearization with dual bounds. Two results are aimed at a management audience: the cost of assuming independence, and the coordination gap between a centralized task force and the same agencies spending separately.

  • 2026
    Multiple non-independent interventions on the same network element: A scoping review
    Under review at Networks

    A PRISMA-ScR review registered on OSF, across four databases with two independent reviewers screening and extracting. Establishes that independence, or the absence of same-element multiplicity altogether, is the norm in the literature.

  • 2026
    Network interdiction to improve labor trafficking detection in the U.S. agricultural sector
    Revise and resubmit at Decision Sciences

    Applies network interdiction to collaborative detection across the Department of Labor and the Department of Homeland Security.

  • 2026
    Structural results and complexity for network interdiction with dependent multiple interventions
    Working paper

    Under a logarithmic transform the problem is shortest-path interdiction and therefore NP-hard. The claim is narrower and more useful: structure discovery is polynomial and independent of the choice of function, and the optimal intervention can be restricted to a candidate set determined by which parts of the network are in play.

  • 2026
    Algorithms for network interdiction problems with nonlinear attacks
    Working paper

    A three-phase procedure — structure recovery, depth determination, and budget allocation with re-optimization after each commitment — verified against exhaustive enumeration on instances small enough to permit it, and reported by structural case.

  • 2026
    Active learning experiences: A teaching fellowship in industrial engineering education
    Working paper, in preparation

    Narrative and autoethnographic analysis of how mentored teaching shapes an instructor’s confidence and identity.

  • 2020
    U.S. ports of entry's decision model with an anti-human trafficking focus
    Decision Sciences Institute Proceedings — refereed conference proceedings

    A Bayesian decision model with cost-benefit analysis for when officers at U.S. ports of entry should escalate a screening for possible human trafficking.
