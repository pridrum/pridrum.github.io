## Course Overview

**Institution:** Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), Porto Alegre, Brazil  
**Role:** Graduate Teaching Assistant  
**Terms:** Fall 2017, Spring 2018, Spring 2019 (Prof. Denise Lindstrom, 30+ students per term)

Manufacturing Production and Control covers production planning, scheduling, inventory, and the
control systems that hold them together.

---

## What I did

I delivered bi-weekly research seminars and gave project feedback on student work carried out with
industry partners.

The industry-partnered projects are the part I took forward into my own teaching. Students were not
working a case written for them. They were working a real problem for a company that would read the
answer, which meant the data was incomplete, the objective was contested, and someone still had to
decide. That is the condition I now build into every course I design.

This was also the period of my own master's work in the same department, evaluating the network of
services for survivors of domestic and gender-based violence across more than 450 municipalities in
Rio Grande do Sul.
